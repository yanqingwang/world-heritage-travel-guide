## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

微山湖是北方最大的淡水湖，是红色革命老区，抗日战争时期，以微山湖为依托，微湖大队、运河支队、铁道游击队战功赫赫。微山湖四季精彩独具，素有“中国荷都，北方水乡，铁道游击队故乡”之美誉。

### English

Weishan Lake is the largest freshwater lake in the north and an old red revolutionary area. During the Anti-Japanese War, relying on Weishan Lake, the Weihu Brigade, Canal Detachment, and Railway Guerrillas made great achievements. Weishan Lake is unique in all four seasons and is known as "the Dutch capital of China, the northern water town, and the hometown of the railway guerrillas".
