# 保亭县呀诺达雨林文化旅游区 / Baotingxianyanuodayulin Cultural Tourism Area

> 海南省 · 保亭黎族苗族自治县 · 主题乐园

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

呀诺达热带雨林景区是比较有观赏价值的热带雨林资源博览馆。景区主要由经石峡、梦幻谷、黎锦苑、山盟海誓、快乐体验、热带瓜果乡、雨林水族馆、欢乐雨林“呀诺达”大型演艺和综合服务区九个主题景观区组成。

### English

Yanoda Tropical Rainforest Scenic Area is a tropical rainforest resource expo with relatively ornamental value. The scenic area is mainly composed of nine themed landscape areas: Jingshi Gorge, Dream Valley, Li Jinyuan, Mountain Alliance, Happy Experience, Tropical Fruit Country, Rainforest Aquarium, and Happy Rainforest "Yanoda" large-scale performance and comprehensive service area.
