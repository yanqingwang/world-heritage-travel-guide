# 保山市火山热海旅游区 / Baoshanshihuoshanrehai Tourist Area

> 云南省 · 保山市 · 度假休闲

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

火山热海位于腾冲县城西南20公里，面积约9平方公里，较大的气泉、温泉群共有80余处，其中有14个温泉群的水温达90℃以上。2016年被国家旅游局批准为国家AAAAA级旅游景区。

### English

The Volcano Hot Sea is located 20 kilometers southwest of Tengchong County, covering an area of ​​about 9 square kilometers. There are more than 80 large gas springs and hot spring groups, among which 14 hot spring groups have water temperatures above 90°C. In 2016, it was approved by the National Tourism Administration as a national AAAAA tourist attraction.
