## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

太阳岛是一处由冰雪文化、民俗文化等资源构成的多功能风景区，也是中国国内的沿江生态区。太阳岛风景区被授予国家级风景名胜区、国家水利风景区、国家湿地公园试点单位等荣誉。

### English

Sun Island is a multi-functional scenic spot composed of ice and snow culture, folk culture and other resources. It is also a riverside ecological zone in China. Sun Island Scenic Area has been awarded the honors of a national scenic spot, a national water conservancy scenic spot, and a national wetland park pilot unit.
