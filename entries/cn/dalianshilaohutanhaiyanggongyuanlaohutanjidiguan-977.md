## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

大连老虎滩海洋公园拥有极地馆、珊瑚馆、鸟语林、海兽馆、欢乐剧场、群虎雕塑，以及马驷骥根雕艺术馆等景点。这里还有全国最长的大型跨海空中索道，在极地动物馆中，有白鲸、海豚、北极熊、海象、企鹅等百余种极地动物。

### English

Dalian Laohutan Ocean Park has attractions such as the Polar Pavilion, Coral Pavilion, Birdsong Forest, Marine Animal Pavilion, Happy Theater, Tiger Sculptures, and Ma Siji Root Sculpture Art Museum. There is also the longest large-scale cross-sea aerial ropeway in the country. In the Polar Animal Museum, there are more than 100 kinds of polar animals such as beluga whales, dolphins, polar bears, walruses, and penguins.
