## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

景区位于漓江之畔，是以山形秀美的独秀峰为代表和整个明代靖江王府为地域范围的景区。景区内自然山水风光与历史人文景观交相辉映，“桂林山水甲天下”这千古名句的真迹题刻就出于此处。景区涵盖了桂林三大历史文化体系，是桂林历史文化的典型代表。

### English

The scenic spot is located on the bank of the Li River. It is represented by the beautiful Duxiu Peak and covers the entire Jingjiang Palace of the Ming Dynasty. The natural landscape and historical and cultural landscape in the scenic area complement each other. The authentic inscription of the ancient famous saying "Guilin's landscape is the best in the world" came from here. The scenic spot covers the three major historical and cultural systems of Guilin and is a typical representative of Guilin's historical culture.
