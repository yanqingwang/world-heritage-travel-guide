## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

衡水湖旅游景区面积26.4平方公里，由享有“双金赛事”的衡水湖马拉松环湖赛道，串联衡水湖生态体验区、冀州古城历史文化区、闾里古镇传统文化研学区以及鸭鸭乐园亲子度假区而成。来衡水湖景区，可泛舟湖上，看碧波万顷，鸟浪翻滚；可徜徉于衡水湖湿地生态馆，赏一幅人与自然和谐相处的历史长卷；可驻足冀州古城遗址，与大儒董子来一次穿越时空的对话；可走进孙敬学堂，学习体验中国传统礼孝文化；可携手家人，在鸭鸭探险岛来一场酣畅淋漓的游戏。

### English

The Hengshui Lake tourist attraction covers an area of ​​26.4 square kilometers. It is composed of the Hengshui Lake Marathon Lake Course, which enjoys the "Double Gold Race", and connects the Hengshui Lake Ecological Experience Area, Jizhou Ancient City Historical and Cultural Area, Luli Ancient Town Traditional Culture Study Area and Yaya Paradise Parent-child Resort. When you come to the Hengshui Lake Scenic Area, you can go boating on the lake to see the vast expanse of blue waves and rolling birds; you can wander in the Hengshui Lake Wetland Ecological Museum and appreciate a long historical scroll of the harmonious coexistence of man and nature; you can stop at the ruins of the ancient city of Jizhou and have a time-traveling conversation with the great Confucian Dong Zi; you can enter the Sun Jing School to learn and experience the traditional Chinese culture of etiquette and filial piety; you can join your family and play a hearty game at the Duck Adventure Island.
