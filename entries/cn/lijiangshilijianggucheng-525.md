# 丽江市丽江古城景区 / Lijianggucheng Scenic Area

> 云南省 · 丽江市 · 古镇村落

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

丽江古城是中国四大古城之一，被列入“世界文化遗产名录”。丽江古城是一座没有城墙的古城，主要由古街、古桥、木府、各种古建筑组成，这里有着多彩的地方民族习俗和娱乐活动包括别具特色的纳西古乐、东巴仪式、占卜文化、古镇酒吧以及纳西族火把节等。

### English

Lijiang Old Town is one of the four ancient cities in China and is included in the "World Cultural Heritage List". Lijiang Old Town is an ancient city without city walls. It is mainly composed of ancient streets, ancient bridges, wooden mansions, and various ancient buildings. It has colorful local ethnic customs and entertainment activities, including unique Naxi ancient music, Dongba rituals, divination culture, ancient town bars, and the Naxi Torch Festival.
