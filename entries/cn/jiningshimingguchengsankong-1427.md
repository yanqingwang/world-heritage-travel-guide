## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

曲阜是孔子的故乡。城中孔府、孔庙、孔林，统称“三孔”，是中国历代纪念孔子，推崇儒学的象征。其中，孔庙是国内最大的祭祀孔子的庙宇，也是全世界孔庙的始祖。三孔景区具有丰厚的文化积淀，丰富的文物珍藏和科学艺术价值。

### English

Qufu is the hometown of Confucius. The Confucius Mansion, Confucius Temple, and Confucius Forest in the city are collectively referred to as the "Three Confucius". They are symbols of the commemoration of Confucius and the admiration of Confucianism in Chinese dynasties. Among them, the Confucius Temple is the largest temple dedicated to Confucius in China and the ancestor of Confucius temples in the world. Sankong Scenic Area has rich cultural accumulation, rich cultural relic collection and scientific and artistic value.
