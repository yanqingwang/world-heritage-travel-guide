## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

青秀山风景区坐落在蜿蜒流淌的邕江畔，是南宁市最靓丽的城市名片之一。景区群峰起伏、林木青翠、岩幽壁峭、泉清石奇，以南亚热带植物景观为特色，常年云雾环绕，具有高浓度的负氧离子，形成了一个独特的天然休闲氧吧，素有“城市绿肺”“绿城翡翠，壮乡凤凰”的美誉。

### English

Qingxiu Mountain Scenic Area is located on the bank of the winding Yongjiang River. It is one of the most beautiful city cards in Nanning. The scenic area has undulating peaks, green trees, steep rocks, clear springs and strange rocks. It is characterized by the south subtropical plant landscape. It is surrounded by clouds and mist all year round and has a high concentration of negative oxygen ions, forming a unique natural leisure oxygen bar. It is known as the "green lung of the city", "emerald city, phoenix city".
