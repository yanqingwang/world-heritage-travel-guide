# 焦作市云台山-神农山-青天河风景区 / Qingtianhe Scenic Area

> 河南省 · 焦作市 · 主题乐园

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

风景区包含云台山、青天河、神农山三个主题景区。其中，云台山是一处以太行山岳水景为特色，以峡谷类地质地貌景观和历史文化为内涵的科普生态旅游景区；神农山相传是道教创始人老子筑炉炼丹、成道仙升之所；青天河是集江南水乡与北国田园风光于一体的旅游景区，素有“北方三峡”之荣和“豫北小桂林”之誉。

### English

The scenic area includes three themed scenic areas: Yuntai Mountain, Qingtian River, and Shennong Mountain. Among them, Yuntai Mountain is a popular science and eco-tourism scenic spot featuring the waterscape of the Taihang Mountains, canyon-like geological landscapes and historical culture; Shennong Mountain is said to be the place where Laozi, the founder of Taoism, built a furnace to refine elixirs and became a Taoist; Qingtian River is a tourist attraction integrating the water towns of the south of the Yangtze River and the pastoral scenery of the North. It is known as the "Three Gorges of the North" and the "Little Guilin of Northern Henan".
