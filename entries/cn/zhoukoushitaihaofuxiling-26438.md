## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

太昊陵,即“三皇之首”太昊伏羲氏的陵庙,位于河南省淮阳区羲皇故都风景名胜区,毗邻风景秀丽的万亩龙湖,是全国重点文物保护单位,中国十八大名陵之一。

### English

Zhoukou (Chinese: 周口; pinyin: Zhōukǒu; Wade–Giles: Chou-k’ou; postal: Chowkow) is a prefecture-level city in eastern Henan province, China. It borders Zhumadian to the southwest, Xuchang and Luohe to the west, Kaifeng to the northwest, Shangqiu to the northeast, and the province of Anhui on all other sides. As of the 2020 census, its population was 9,026,015 inhabitants. However, as of the 2018 estimation, 1,601,300 lived in the built-up (or metro) made up of Chuanhui district and the northern part of Shangshui county. The history and geography of Zhoukou have made it a city. As the ancient capitals of Emperor Yao and the birthplace of Laozi, it carries a cultural heritage of China. At the same time, the remnants of the Chen and Chu cultures endow Zhoukou with historical significance. The development of the Lin'gang New City reflects the city's modernization process and economic development. The convergence of the Sha Ying rivers has endowed Zhoukou with unparalleled geographical advantages, making it a hub known as the Central Plains Port City.
