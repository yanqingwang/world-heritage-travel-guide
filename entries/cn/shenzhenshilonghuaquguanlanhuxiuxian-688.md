# 深圳市龙华区观澜湖休闲旅游区 / Shenzhen railway station

> 广东省 · 深圳市 · 宗教文化

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

旅游区集运动休闲、商务休闲、养生休闲、会议旅游、文化娱乐、美食购物、长居短憩七大功能为一体，其中包括观澜湖高尔夫球会、网球乡村俱乐部、多家酒店、国际会议中心、生态休憩园，以及丰富的运动、餐饮、文娱、游乐等设施。

### English

Shenzhen railway station (Chinese: 深圳站; pinyin: Shēnzhèn zhàn, formerly Shum Chun station), also unofficially known as Luohu railway station (Chinese: 罗湖站; pinyin: Luóhú zhàn), is located across from Luohu Commercial City in Nanhu Subdistrict, Luohu District of Shenzhen, Guangdong. It is the southern terminus of the Guangzhou-Shenzhen Railway. It is one of the two stations with high-speed rail service in Luohu District. The other station is Luohu North railway station, which is currently under construction on Shenzhen–Shanwei high-speed railway.
