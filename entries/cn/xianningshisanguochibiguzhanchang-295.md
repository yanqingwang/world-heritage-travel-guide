# 咸宁市三国赤壁古战场景区 / Chibi Scenic Area

> 湖北省 · 咸宁市 · 石窟艺术

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

三国赤壁古战场，相传是赤壁之战发生地。主要景观有赤壁摩崖石刻、周瑜塑像、拜风台、凤雏庵、翼江亭、赤壁大战陈列馆、赤壁碑廊、千年银杏、三国雕塑园等。

### English

The ancient battlefield of Chibi in the Three Kingdoms is said to be the place where the Battle of Chibi took place. The main landscapes include Chibi Cliff Carvings, Statue of Zhou Yu, Wind Worship Platform, Fengchu Nunnery, Yijiang Pavilion, Chibi War Exhibition Hall, Chibi Stele Gallery, Millennium Ginkgo, Three Kingdoms Sculpture Garden, etc.
