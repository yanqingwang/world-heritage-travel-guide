# 陇南市官鹅沟景区 / Longnanshiguanegou Scenic Area

> 甘肃省 · 陇南市 · 地质奇观

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

官鹅沟景区位于陇南市西北部，地处青藏高原边缘岷山山系与西秦岭延伸交错地带，总面积66平方公里，由官珠沟、鹅嫚沟和雷古山三大游览片区组成，集森林湖泊、峡谷瀑布和高山草甸等景观于一体。

### English

Guanyegou Scenic Area is located in the northwest of Longnan City, at the edge of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau in the intersecting zone of the Minshan Mountains and the West Qinling Mountains. It covers a total area of ​​66 square kilometers and consists of three major tourist areas: Guanzhu Valley, Emanggou and Leigu Mountain. It integrates forest lakes, canyon waterfalls and alpine meadows.
