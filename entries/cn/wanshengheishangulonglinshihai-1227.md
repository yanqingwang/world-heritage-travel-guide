# 万盛黑山谷-龙鳞石海风景区 / Wanshengdongzhan (chongqingshi) Scenic Area

> 重庆市 · 綦江县 · 地质奇观

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

黑山谷景区由黑山谷、龙鳞石海两个景区组成，有峻岭、峰林、幽峡、峭壁、森林、竹海、飞瀑、碧水、溶洞、仿古栈道、浮桥、云海、田园、原始植被、珍稀动植物等景观。景区保存着地球上同纬度为数不多的亚热带和温带完好的自然生态，是目前重庆地区最大的、原始生态保护最为完好的自然生态风景区。

### English

The Black Valley Scenic Area consists of two scenic areas, the Black Valley and the Dragon Scale Stone Sea. It has landscapes such as steep mountains, peak forests, secluded gorges, cliffs, forests, bamboo seas, waterfalls, clear water, caves, antique plank roads, floating bridges, seas of clouds, pastoral fields, primitive vegetation, rare animals and plants, etc. The scenic spot preserves one of the few intact subtropical and temperate natural ecology at the same latitude on the earth. It is currently the largest natural ecological scenic spot in Chongqing with the best original ecological protection.
