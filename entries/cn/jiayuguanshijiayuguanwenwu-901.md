## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

嘉峪关是明代万里长城最西端的关口，历史上曾被称为“河西咽喉”，因地势险要，建筑雄伟，有“天下第一雄关”“连陲锁钥”之称。嘉峪关始建于公元1372年，由内城、外城、罗城、瓮城、城壕和南北两翼长城组成，全长约60公里。

### English

Jiayu Pass or (simplified Chinese: 嘉峪关; traditional Chinese: 嘉峪關; pinyin: Jiāyù Guān) is the first frontier fortress at the west end of the Ming dynasty Great Wall, near the city of Jiayuguan in Gansu province. Along with Juyong Pass and Shanhai Pass, it is one of the main passes of the Great Wall. In the Ming period, foreign merchants and envoys from Central Asia and West Asia mostly entered China through Jiayu Pass.
