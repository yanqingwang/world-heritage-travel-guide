# 南通市濠河风景区 / Haohe Scenic Area

> 江苏省 · 南通市 · 自然风光

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

濠河位于国家历史文化名城南通的中心，是国内保存最为完整的古护城河之一。整个濠河风景区绿树如荫，风光秀美，是独具“城包水、水包城、城水一体”格局，汇自然景观与人文景观于一身的环城敞开式景区。

### English

Haohe River is located in the center of Nantong, a national historical and cultural city. It is one of the most complete ancient moats in China. The entire Haohe Scenic Area is full of green trees and beautiful scenery. It is an open scenic area around the city with a unique pattern of "city surrounded by water, water surrounding the city, and city and water integrated", integrating natural and cultural landscapes.
