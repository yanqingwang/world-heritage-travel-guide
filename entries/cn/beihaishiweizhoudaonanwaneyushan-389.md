# 北海市涠洲岛南湾鳄鱼山景区 / Beihaishiweizhoudaonanwaneyushan Scenic Area

> 广西壮族自治区 · 北海市 · 红色旅游

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

涠洲岛是火山喷发堆凝而成的岛屿，是中国地质年龄最年轻的火山岛，也是广西最大的海岛。南湾鳄鱼山景区是涠洲岛的精华景区，以美丽的海岛风光、典型的火山地质遗迹、奇特的地貌景观和舒适宜人的气候闻名海内外，包含鳄鱼山、五彩滩、红色广场、南湾海洋运动公园等景点。

### English

Weizhou Island is an island formed by volcanic eruptions. It is the youngest volcanic island in China and the largest island in Guangxi. Nanwan Crocodile Mountain Scenic Area is the essence of Weizhou Island. It is famous at home and abroad for its beautiful island scenery, typical volcanic geological relics, unique landforms and comfortable and pleasant climate. It includes Crocodile Mountain, Colorful Beach, Red Square, Nanwan Ocean Sports Park and other attractions.
