## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

凤凰古城旅游区由凤凰古城和南华山两个区域组成，总面积4.39平方公里，是以青山抱江的山水格局为基础，以史迹荟萃的人文古城为核心，以浓郁湘西民族风情为特色的旅游胜地。凤凰古城始建于唐，繁荣于明清，古城街巷自由舒展，空间灵活多变，文化底蕴厚重。民国内阁总理熊希龄、一代文豪沈从文、著名画家黄永玉等都出生在这里。凤凰古城属国家级历史文化名城，被誉为“中国最美的小城”。

### English

Fenghuang Ancient City Tourist Area consists of two areas: Fenghuang Ancient City and Nanhua Mountain, with a total area of ​​4.39 square kilometers. It is a tourist attraction based on the landscape pattern of green mountains and rivers, with the cultural ancient city with a collection of historical sites as the core, and the rich ethnic customs of western Hunan. Fenghuang Ancient City was built in the Tang Dynasty and flourished in the Ming and Qing Dynasties. The streets and alleys of the ancient city stretch freely, the space is flexible and changeable, and the cultural heritage is rich. Xiong Xiling, Prime Minister of the Republic of China, Shen Congwen, a generation of literary giants, and Huang Yongyu, a famous painter, were all born here. Fenghuang Ancient City is a national historical and cultural city and is known as "the most beautiful small town in China".
