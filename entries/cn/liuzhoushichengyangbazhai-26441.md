## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

程阳八寨景区位于广西唯一的侗族自治县——三江侗族自治县境内，由马鞍、平寨、岩寨、平坦、懂寨、大寨、平铺、吉昌八个自然村寨组成，上万侗族人世居于此，创造了丰富多彩、灿烂辉煌的侗族文化。

### English

Chengyang Bazhai Scenic Area is located in Sanjiang Dong Autonomous County, the only Dong autonomous county in Guangxi. It consists of eight natural villages: Ma'an, Pingzhai, Yanzhai, Pingping, Dangzhai, Dazhai, Pingpu and Jichang. Tens of thousands of Dong people have lived here, creating a rich, colorful and splendid Dong culture.
