# 牡丹江市镜泊湖景区 / Mudanjiangshijingpohu Scenic Area

> 黑龙江省 · 牡丹江市 · 度假休闲

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

镜泊湖国家级风景名胜区，由百里长湖景区、火山口原始森林景区、渤海国上京龙泉府遗址景区三部分组成。景区以湖光山色为主，兼有火山口地下原始森林、地下熔岩隧道等地质奇观及唐代渤海国遗址为代表的历史人文景观，是可供科研、避暑、游览、观光、度假和文化交流的综合性景区。

### English

Jingbo Lake National Scenic Area consists of three parts: the Baili Long Lake Scenic Area, the Crater Primitive Forest Scenic Area, and the Bohai State Shangjing Longquan Mansion Ruins Scenic Area. The scenic spot is dominated by lakes and mountains, as well as geological wonders such as the virgin forest under the crater and underground lava tunnels, as well as historical and cultural landscapes represented by the Bohai Kingdom ruins of the Tang Dynasty. It is a comprehensive scenic spot for scientific research, summer vacation, sightseeing, vacation and cultural exchanges.
