# 长沙市花明楼景区 / Changshashihuaminglou Scenic Area

> 湖南省 · 长沙市 · 历史遗址

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

花明楼是一处低山环绕的地方，这里不仅有秀美的自然风光，还是刘少奇同志的故居。花明楼景区共占地690多亩，包括刘少奇故居、刘少奇纪念馆、刘少奇同志铜像广场、花明楼城楼、文物馆、柳叶湖和修养亭等景点。

### English

Huaminglou is a place surrounded by low mountains. Not only does it have beautiful natural scenery, it is also the former residence of Comrade Liu Shaoqi. The Huaming Tower Scenic Area covers a total area of ​​more than 690 acres, including Liu Shaoqi's former residence, Liu Shaoqi Memorial Hall, Comrade Liu Shaoqi Bronze Statue Square, Huaming Tower Tower, Cultural Relics Museum, Liuye Lake and Xiuyu Pavilion.
