## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

明显陵文化旅游景区，位于享有世界长寿之乡、国家级历史文化名城、中国优秀旅游城市等殊荣的湖北省钟祥市城东郊，景区面积3.18平方公里。景区包括世界文化遗产明显陵、华中民俗第一村莫愁村、国家级湿地公园莫愁湖和明代帝王文化展示馆，是集水域风光、园林景观、人文历史、古建筑为一体的半开放式景区，山水林陵资源组合极佳。

### English

Mingmingling Cultural Tourism Scenic Area is located in the eastern suburbs of Zhongxiang City, Hubei Province, which has the honor of being the hometown of longevity in the world, a national historical and cultural city, and an outstanding tourist city in China. The scenic area covers an area of ​​3.18 square kilometers. The scenic area includes the Ming Ming Mausoleum, a world cultural heritage site, Mochou Village, the first folk village in Central China, Mochou Lake, a national wetland park, and the Ming Dynasty Emperor Culture Exhibition Hall. It is a semi-open scenic area that integrates water scenery, garden landscapes, humanities, history, and ancient buildings. It has an excellent combination of mountains, rivers, forests, and tombs.
