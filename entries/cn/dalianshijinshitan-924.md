## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

金石滩是国家级风景名胜区、国家级旅游度假区、国家级地质公园。景区三面环海，冬暖夏凉，气候宜人。主要景点有黄金海岸、金石文化博览广场、滨海国家地质公园、金石缘公园、金石蜡像馆、发现王国等。

### English

Jinshitan is a national-level scenic spot, a national-level tourist resort, and a national-level geological park. The scenic spot is surrounded by the sea on three sides, with warm winters and cool summers, and a pleasant climate. The main attractions include Gold Coast, Jinshi Culture Expo Plaza, Binhai National Geopark, Jinshiyuan Park, Jinshi Paraffin Museum, Discovery Kingdom, etc.
