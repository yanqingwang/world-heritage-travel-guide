## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

周村古商城景区位于山东省淄博市周村区，素有“旱码头”“金周村”“丝绸之乡”的美誉。景区总占地面积60.5公顷，主要由大街、丝市街、银子市街等古街组成，现有保存完好的明清古建筑5万余平方米。周村古商城历经数百年风雨至今仍保留完好，街区纵横，店铺林立，建筑风格迥异，被中国古建筑保护委员会的专家誉为“中国活着的古商业建筑博物馆群”，具有很高的旅游观光价值。

### English

Zibo (Chinese: 淄博; pinyin: Zībó) is a prefecture-level city in central Shandong province, China. It borders the provincial capital Jinan to the west, Tai'an to the southwest, Linyi to the south, Weifang to the east, Dongying to the northeast, and Binzhou to the north. Zibo spans 5,938 square kilometres (2,293 sq mi). As of the 2020 census, Zibo's population was 4,704,138, of which 4.41 million lived in the metro area comprising five urban districts—Zhangdian, Zichuan, Boshan, Zhoucun and Linzi–and parts of neighboring counties Huantai, Gaoqing, and Yiyuan. The Zibo area was the centre of the ancient State of Qi, whose capital Linzi was the most populous city in China at its peak. Pu Songling, a well-known writer of the Qing dynasty, is one of the most famous people from Zibo. As the birthplace of Qi culture, Zibo is a notable tourist city. Manufacturingholds an important place in the city’s economy, particularly ceramics manufacturing. Other key industries include the petrochemical industry, pharmaceuticals, metallurgy, construction materials, machinery and textiles.
