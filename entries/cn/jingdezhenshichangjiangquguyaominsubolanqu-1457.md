# 景德镇市昌江区古窑民俗博览区 / Jingdezhenguyaominsubolanqu

> 江西省 · 景德镇市 · 主题乐园

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

景德镇古窑民俗博览区占地83公顷，是集文化博览、陶瓷体验、娱乐休闲为一体的文化旅游景区，是一家以陶瓷文化为主题的国家级旅游景区。主要景点有历代古窑展示区、陶瓷民俗展示区、水岸前街创意休憩区三大景区。

### English

Jingdezhen Ancient Kiln Folklore Expo Area covers an area of ​​83 hectares. It is a cultural tourist attraction integrating cultural exposition, ceramic experience, entertainment and leisure. It is a national tourist attraction with the theme of ceramic culture. The main attractions include the ancient kiln exhibition area, the ceramic folk custom exhibition area, and the Shui'an Qian Street creative rest area.
