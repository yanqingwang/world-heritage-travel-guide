## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

清明上河园是一座大型宋代文化实景主题公园，坐落在开封市龙亭湖西岸，是中国非物质文化遗产展演基地。该景区是以画家张择端的写实画作《清明上河图》为蓝本，按照《营造法式》为建设标准，以宋朝市井文化、民俗风情、皇家园林和古代娱乐为题材，以游客参与体验为特点的文化主题公园。

### English

Qingming Riverside Garden is a large-scale Song Dynasty cultural real-life theme park located on the west bank of Longting Lake in Kaifeng City. It is a performance base for China's intangible cultural heritage. The scenic spot is a cultural theme park based on the realistic painting "Along the River During the Qingming Festival" by the painter Zhang Zeduan, in accordance with the "Building Method" as the construction standard, with themes of Song Dynasty street culture, folk customs, royal gardens and ancient entertainment, and featuring tourist participation and experience.
