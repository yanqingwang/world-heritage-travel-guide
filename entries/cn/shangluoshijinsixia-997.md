# 商洛市金丝峡景区 / Jinsixia Scenic Area

> 陕西省 · 商洛市 · 地质奇观

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

景区内有白龙峡、黑龙峡、青龙峡、石燕寨和丹江源五大景区。金丝峡以窄、幽、秀、奇而闻名，河流密布，森林茂密，野生动植物繁多，原始生态保存完好，是秦岭地区最完整的嶂谷地质构造。

### English

There are five major scenic spots in the scenic area: Bailong Gorge, Heilong Gorge, Qinglong Gorge, Shiyanzhai and Danjiangyuan. Jinsi Gorge is famous for being narrow, secluded, beautiful and strange, with dense rivers, dense forests, abundant wild animals and plants, and well-preserved original ecology. It is the most complete Zhanggu geological structure in the Qinling Mountains.
