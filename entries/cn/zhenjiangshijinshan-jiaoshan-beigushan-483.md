# 镇江市金山•焦山•北固山风景区 / Zhenjiangshijinshan•jiaoshan•beigushanfeng Scenic Area

> 江苏省 · 镇江市 · 山地风光

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

镇江三山，即金山、北固山和焦山，沿江屹立，风景秀丽壮观，犹如一幅壮丽的山水长卷。金山名胜古迹甚多，俯拾皆是。焦山原名樵山，岿然耸立扬子江心，与对岸象山夹江对峙。北固山以险峻著称，甘露寺高踞峰颠，形成“寺冠山”特色。

### English

The three mountains of Zhenjiang, namely Jinshan Mountain, Beigu Mountain and Jiaoshan Mountain, stand along the river, with beautiful and spectacular scenery, like a magnificent landscape scroll. There are many places of interest and historical sites in Jinshan, which can be found everywhere. Jiaoshan, formerly known as Qiaoshan, stands firmly in the middle of the Yangtze River, confronting Xiangshan and Jiajiang on the other side. Beigu Mountain is famous for its steepness. Ganlu Temple is located on the top of the mountain, forming the characteristic of "Temple Crown Mountain".
