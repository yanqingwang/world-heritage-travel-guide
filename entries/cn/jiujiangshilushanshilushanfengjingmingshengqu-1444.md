## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

庐山风景名胜区是世界文化遗产、世界地质公园、国家重点风景名胜区、全国重点文物保护单位，也是避暑疗养胜地。庐山以雄、奇、险、秀闻名，素有“匡庐奇秀甲天下”之美誉。

### English

Lushan City (simplified Chinese: 庐山市; traditional Chinese: 廬山市; pinyin: Lúshān), formerly Xingzi County (Chinese: 星子县), is a county-level city in the north of Jiangxi Province, China. It is under the administration of the prefecture-level city of Jiujiang.
