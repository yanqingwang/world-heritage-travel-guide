# 文山州普者黑旅游景区 / Wenshanzhoupuzhehei Scenic Area

> 云南省 · 文山壮族苗族自治州 · 古镇村落

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

景区总面积388平方公里，核心景区165平方公里，属于滇东南岩溶区，是发育典型的喀斯特岩溶地貌，以“水上田园、湖泊峰林、彝家水乡、岩溶湿地、荷花世界、候鸟天堂”六大景观而著称。

### English

The total area of ​​the scenic area is 388 square kilometers, and the core scenic area is 165 square kilometers. It belongs to the karst area in southeastern Yunnan and is a typical karst landform. It is famous for its six major landscapes: "water pastoral, lake peak forest, Yijia water town, karst wetland, lotus world, and migratory bird paradise".
