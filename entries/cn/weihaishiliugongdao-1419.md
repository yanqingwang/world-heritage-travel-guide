## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

刘公岛是北洋水师的诞生地和甲午战争的古战场。岛上自然风光优美，植物茂密，松涛翠柏，郁郁葱葱；岛内人文景观丰富独特，有上溯千年的战国遗址、下至清朝北洋海军提督署、水师学堂、古炮台等甲午战争遗址，还有众多英租时期遗留下来的欧式建筑。

### English

Liugong Island (simplified Chinese: 刘公岛; traditional Chinese: 劉公島; pinyin: Liúgōng Dǎo; Wade–Giles: Liu-kung Tao) is a small island located on the northeastern edge of Shandong Peninsula, China, at the mouth of Weihai Bay. It is known as the "birthplace of China's first modern navy" and is also the site of its defeat in the First Sino-Japanese War.
