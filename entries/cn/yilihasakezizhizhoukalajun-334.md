# 伊犁哈萨克自治州喀拉峻景区 / Yilihasakezizhizhoukalajun Scenic Area

> 新疆维吾尔自治区 · 伊犁哈萨克自治州 · 草原沙漠

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

景区内雪峰与峡谷辉映，森林与草原镶嵌，春夏季五花绽放，姹紫嫣红，晨曦落霞映照下线条柔美、层次分明，构成一幅幅艺术画卷。喀拉峻景区作为“新疆天山”世界自然遗产的重要组成部分，是新疆天山生物多样性较丰富、美学价值较高的区域。

### English

In the scenic area, snow peaks and canyons are reflected, forests and grasslands are inlaid, and flowers bloom in spring and summer, with colorful lines and soft lines and clear layers reflected by the morning sun and sunset, forming an artistic picture. As an important part of the "Xinjiang Tianshan" World Natural Heritage, the Kalajun Scenic Area is an area with rich biological diversity and high aesthetic value in the Xinjiang Tianshan Mountains.
