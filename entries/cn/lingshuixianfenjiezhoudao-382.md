## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

分界洲岛旅游区具有热带海岛风情特色，当地特殊的地理位置、气候特征及地域文化吸引众多新人来这里举办浪漫的海底婚礼。

### English

Boundary Island (Chinese:分界洲) is an island in Lingshui County, Hainan, China, located just off the south-east coast of Hainan Island, between Lingshui and Wanning, in Riyue Bay at 18.57864°N 110.195381°E﻿ / 18.57864; 110.195381. It is the earliest development of an open uninhabited tourist type area. In 2008 it became a national 4A level scenic spot, and in 2013 was promoted to national 5A-class tourist attraction, becoming China's first.
