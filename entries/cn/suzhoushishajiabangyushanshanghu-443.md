# 苏州市沙家浜-虞山尚湖旅游区 / Yushanlinchang Tourist Area

> 江苏省 · 苏州市 · 红色旅游

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

虞山是国家森林公园，是中国吴文化的重要发源地。尚湖与虞山相依，湖内湿地遍布，鹭鸟翔飞。沙家浜以京剧《沙家浜》而闻名，形成了革命传统教育区、红石民俗文化村、横泾老街、湿地公园等景点，以红色教育游、绿色生态游、金色美食游、影视文化游为特色。

### English

Yushan is a national forest park and an important birthplace of Wu culture in China. Shanghu Lake and Yushan Mountain depend on each other. There are wetlands all over the lake and herons are flying around. Shajiabang is famous for the Peking Opera "Shajiabang" and has formed revolutionary traditional education area, Hongshi Folk Culture Village, Hengjing Old Street, Wetland Park and other attractions, featuring red educational tours, green ecological tours, golden food tours, and film and television cultural tours.
