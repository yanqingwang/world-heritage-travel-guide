## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

万峰林景区位于贵州省兴义市西南侧，地处云贵高原东南部边缘的黔西南腹地，东经 104°51' 至 104°58' ，北纬 24°58' 至 25°42' 之间。南端与广西交界，西到滇、桂、黔三省（区）交界处的三江口，北接乌蒙山主峰，山峰高点海拔 1600 米，平均海拔 1200 米，森林覆盖率 68.5% ，总面积达 2000 多平方公里，是世界锥状喀斯特峰林地貌发育最典型的区域。万峰林景区以发育三叠纪地层岩石的圆锥状峰林、峰丛景观为主，其间分布喀斯特溶洞、漏斗、地下暗河、落水洞以及沉积地层等各类地质遗迹景观。

### English

Wanfenglin Scenic Area is located on the southwest side of Xingyi City, Guizhou Province, in the hinterland of southwestern Guizhou on the southeastern edge of the Yunnan-Guizhou Plateau, between 104°51' and 104°58' east longitude and 24°58' and 25°42' north latitude. It borders Guangxi in the south, Sanjiangkou at the junction of Yunnan, Guangxi, and Guizhou provinces in the west, and the main peak of Wumeng Mountain in the north. The peak of the mountain is 1,600 meters above sea level, with an average altitude of 1,200 meters. It has a forest coverage rate of 68.5% and a total area of ​​more than 2,000 square kilometers. It is the most typical area in the world for the development of conical karst peak forest landforms. The Wanfenglin Scenic Area is dominated by conical peak forests and peak clusters with Triassic stratigraphic rocks. Various geological heritage landscapes such as karst caves, funnels, underground rivers, sinkholes, and sedimentary strata are distributed among them.
