## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

白水洋，位于福建省宁德市屏南县，总面积达77.34平方公里，拥有世界唯一的鸳鸯猕猴自然保护区。 鸳鸯溪国家级风景名胜区位于屏南县东北部，总面积78.8平方公里，是中国唯一的鸳鸯鸟保护区。 每年秋季有数成百上千鸳鸯从北方飞来越冬，这一带早在一百多年前就发现鸳鸯，故有"鸳鸯之乡"的美誉。

### English

Baishuiyang is located in Pingnan County, Ningde City, Fujian Province, with a total area of ​​77.34 square kilometers. It has the world's only mandarin duck and macaque nature reserve. Yuanyangxi National Scenic Area is located in the northeast of Pingnan County, with a total area of ​​78.8 square kilometers. It is the only mandarin duck bird sanctuary in China. Every autumn, hundreds of mandarin ducks fly from the north to spend the winter. Mandarin ducks were discovered in this area more than a hundred years ago, so it has the reputation of "the hometown of mandarin ducks".
