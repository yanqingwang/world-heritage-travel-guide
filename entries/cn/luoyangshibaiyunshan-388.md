## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

洛阳白云山风景区位于河南省洛阳市嵩县南部伏牛山腹地，是世界地质公园 、国家级森林公园、国家级自然保护区。目前已开发白云峰、玉皇顶、鸡角曼、九龙瀑布、原始森林五大观光区和白云湖、高山森林氧吧、高山牡丹园、留侯祠、芦花谷五大休闲区。

### English

Luoyang Baiyun Mountain Scenic Area is located in the hinterland of Funiu Mountain in the south of Song County, Luoyang City, Henan Province. It is a world geological park, a national forest park, and a national nature reserve. At present, five major tourist areas have been developed: Baiyun Peak, Yuhuangding, Jijiaoman, Jiulong Waterfall, and virgin forest, and five major leisure areas: Baiyun Lake, Alpine Forest Oxygen Bar, Alpine Peony Garden, Liuhou Temple, and Luhua Valley.
