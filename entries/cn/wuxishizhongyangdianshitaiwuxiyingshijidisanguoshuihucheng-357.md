# 无锡市中央电视台无锡影视基地三国水浒城景区 / Wuxiyingshijidi Scenic Area

> 江苏省 · 无锡市 · 自然风光

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

中央电视台无锡影视基地是中国首创的大规模影视拍摄和旅游基地。无锡影视基地始建于1987年，占地面积近100公顷，可使用太湖水面200公顷。当年中央电视台为拍摄电视连续剧《唐明皇》、《三国演义》和《水浒传》，相继建成了唐城、三国城和水浒城三大景区。

### English

CCTV Wuxi Film and Television Base is China's first large-scale film and television shooting and tourism base. Wuxi Film and Television Base was founded in 1987, covering an area of ​​nearly 100 hectares and having access to 200 hectares of Taihu Lake water surface. At that time, CCTV built three major scenic spots: Tang City, Three Kingdoms City and Water Margin in order to film the TV series "Tang Minghuang", "Romance of the Three Kingdoms" and "Water Margin".
