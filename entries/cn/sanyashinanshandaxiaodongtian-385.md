## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

三亚大小洞天是著名的道教文化风景区。景区有“小洞天”“钓台”“海山奇观”“仙人足”“试剑峰”等历代诗文摩崖石刻，以其奇特秀丽的海景、山景、石景与洞景被誉为“琼崖八百年第一山水名胜”。

### English

Sanya Dongtian is a famous Taoist cultural scenic spot. The scenic area has cliff stone carvings of poems and articles from past dynasties such as "Little Cave Sky", "Diaotai", "Sea and Mountain Wonders", "Immortal's Foot" and "Testing Sword Peak". It is known as "Qiongya's No. 8 Landscape Scenic Spot in 800 Years" for its unique and beautiful sea views, mountain views, stone views and cave views.
