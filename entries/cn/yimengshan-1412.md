## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

沂蒙山旅游区面积148平方公里，主要由沂山风景区、云蒙景区、龟蒙景区三大景区组成，区域内自然风光秀丽，兼有泰山之雄壮、黄山之秀美、华山之险峻、雁荡山之奇绝。

### English

The Yimeng Mountain Tourist Area covers an area of ​​148 square kilometers and is mainly composed of three major scenic areas: Yishan Scenic Area, Yunmeng Scenic Area, and Guimeng Scenic Area. The natural scenery in the area is beautiful, including the majesty of Mount Tai, the beauty of Mount Huang, the steepness of Mount Huashan, and the wonder of Yandang Mountain.
