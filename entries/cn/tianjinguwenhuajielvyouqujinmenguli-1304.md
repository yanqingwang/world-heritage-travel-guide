## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

天津古文化街又称津门故里，以经营文化用品为主。古文化街内有近百家店堂。是天津老字号店民间手工艺品店的集中地，有狗不理包子、耳朵眼炸糕、煎饼果子、老翟药糖、天津麻花等地道美食，还有天后宫、泥人张彩塑等景点。

### English

Guwenhua Jie, Tianjin's Ancient Culture Street, is a pedestrian pathway complex dotted with temple gates and kiosks on the west bank of the Hai River in Tianjin, China. The Nankai District area is classified as a AAAAA scenic area by the China National Tourism Administration. Tianjin Ancient Culture Street was opened on New Year's Day in 1986. It preserves the architectural style of Qing dynasty, with its Niangniang Palace honoring the sea-goddess Mazu in the center. There are many tourist attractions along the street.
