## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

三峡大瀑布位于长江三峡国家地质公园范围内，由三峡大瀑布和金狮洞两个景区组成，总面积24平方公里。虎口瀑、珍珠瀑、五叠瀑、龙潭、水袈裟大佛等数十个自然瀑布组成了瀑布群，最壮观的是高102米、宽80米的主瀑布。

### English

The Three Gorges Falls is located within the Yangtze Three Gorges National Geopark. It consists of two scenic spots: the Three Gorges Falls and the Golden Lion Cave, with a total area of ​​24 square kilometers. Dozens of natural waterfalls such as Hukou Waterfall, Pearl Waterfall, Wudie Waterfall, Longtan, and Water Kasaya Buddha form a waterfall group. The most spectacular one is the main waterfall that is 102 meters high and 80 meters wide.
