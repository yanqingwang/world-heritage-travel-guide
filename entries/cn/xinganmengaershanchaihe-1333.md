# 兴安盟阿尔山-柴河旅游景区 / Aershanlinyeju Scenic Area

> 内蒙古自治区 · 呼伦贝尔市 · 山地风光

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

阿尔山·柴河景区位于内蒙古东部经济较发达地区的核心位置，与周边城市之间交通便捷，整合了满洲里、海拉尔、扎兰屯、乌兰浩特、锡林浩特等地的旅游资源，构建了内蒙古旅游黄金区域的战略位置。

### English

Aershan·Chaihe Scenic Area is located at the core of the economically developed area in eastern Inner Mongolia. It has convenient transportation with surrounding cities. It integrates the tourism resources of Manzhouli, Hailar, Zhalantun, Ulanhot, Xilinhot and other places, establishing a strategic position as a prime tourism area in Inner Mongolia.
