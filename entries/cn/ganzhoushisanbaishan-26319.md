## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

三百山景区位于江西省安远县境内,地处赣、粤、闽三省交界处，是国家级风景名胜区、国家森林公园。景区总面积197平方公里，“源头群瀑、三百群峰、峡谷险滩、高山平湖、原始林海、火山地貌”堪称三百山六绝，拥有福鳌塘、蝴蝶大峡谷（九曲溪）、东风湖、仰天湖、尖峰笔等5大游览区域。

### English

Sanbai Mountain Scenic Area is located in Anyuan County, Jiangxi Province, at the junction of Jiangxi, Guangdong and Fujian provinces. It is a national scenic spot and a national forest park. The scenic area has a total area of ​​197 square kilometers. The "source waterfalls, 300 peaks, canyons and dangerous shoals, high mountains and flat lakes, primitive forests and seas, and volcanic landforms" are called the 300 Mountains and 6 unique places. It has 5 major tourist areas including Fuao Pond, Butterfly Grand Canyon (Jiuqu River), Dongfeng Lake, Yangtian Lake, and Jianfengbi.
