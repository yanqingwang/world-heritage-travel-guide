# 忻州市五台山风景名胜区 / Xinzhoushiwutaishan Scenic and Historic Area

> 山西省 · 忻州市 · 宗教文化

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

五台山是中国修建佛教建筑最早的地方之一，佛光寺和南禅寺是中国现存最早的两座木质结构建筑。主要景点有塔院寺、菩萨顶、佛光寺、五爷庙、显通寺、万佛阁等。

### English

Mount Wutai is one of the earliest places to build Buddhist buildings in China. Foguang Temple and Nanchan Temple are the two earliest existing wooden structures in China. The main attractions include Tayuan Temple, Bodhisattva Peak, Foguang Temple, Wuye Temple, Xiantong Temple, Wanfo Pavilion, etc.
