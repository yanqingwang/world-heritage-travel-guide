Shenxianju has a unique geological structure. It is a large-scale volcanic rhyolite landform cluster in the world. Every mountain, water, cliff, cave, stone and peak is unique in its own way, forming six wonders: Guanyin, Tathagata, Tianmu Peak, Sea of ​​Clouds, Waterfall, and Tadpole Wen.
