# 海北州阿咪东索景区 / Haibeizhouamidongsuo Scenic Area

> 青海省 · 海北藏族自治州 · 山地风光

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

阿咪东索景区是祁连山的支脉—托来山的主峰。景区占地面积112.97平方公里，核心面积12.47平方公里，高度在2800米到3500米之间。整个景区似一个东西走向的巨大山谷，山谷全长14公里，谷间流水潺潺，云蒸雾蔚，野生植物众多，原始生态保存完整。

### English

Ami Dongsuo Scenic Area is the main peak of Tuolai Mountain, a branch of the Qilian Mountains. The scenic area covers an area of ​​112.97 square kilometers, with a core area of ​​12.47 square kilometers and a height between 2800 meters and 3500 meters. The entire scenic spot is like a huge valley running from east to west, with a total length of 14 kilometers. The valley has gurgling water, steaming clouds and mist, numerous wild plants, and the original ecology is intact.
