## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

苏州吴中太湖旅游区位于天堂苏州西南隅的太湖之滨，境内有中国碧螺春之乡东山景区，穹窿山景区和旺山景区。古镇、古村、古岛等历史人文景观，宛如点缀在太湖山水间的颗颗明珠。

### English

Suzhou Wuzhong Taihu Lake Tourist Area is located on the shore of Taihu Lake in the southwest corner of Paradise Suzhou. Within its territory are Dongshan Scenic Area, Qionglong Mountain Scenic Area and Wangshan Scenic Area, the Hometown of Biluochun in China. Historical and cultural landscapes such as ancient towns, ancient villages, and ancient islands are like pearls dotted among the mountains and rivers of Taihu Lake.
