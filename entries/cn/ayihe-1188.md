# 阿依河景区 / Ayihe Scenic Area

> 重庆市 · 彭水苗族土家族自治县 · 古镇村落

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

阿依河发源于贵州省务川县分水乡，向东北蜿蜒而入重庆市彭水县境内。乘舟而下，沿途可见各种各样的峡谷地貌：有貌似罗汉、状若擎天的石笋，有深不可测的溶洞，还有嬉戏的猴群和庄严的石佛。整个阿依河彭水河段水体资源极为丰富，其中尤以七里塘河段和儿塘河河段的水体景观最为独特，江面绿水清幽，两岸翠竹环绕。

### English

The Ayi River originates from Fenshui Township, Wuchuan County, Guizhou Province, and winds northeastward into Pengshui County, Chongqing City. When you take a boat down, you can see various canyon landforms along the way: there are stalagmites that look like Arhats and towering into the sky, unfathomable caves, playful monkeys and solemn stone Buddhas. The entire Pengshui section of the Ayi River is extremely rich in water resources. Among them, the Qilitang section and the Ertang section have the most unique water landscapes. The river is green and quiet, surrounded by green bamboo on both sides.
