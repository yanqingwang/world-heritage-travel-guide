# 黔东南州镇远县镇远古城旅游景区 / Tianhougong (zhenyuanxian) Scenic Area

> 贵州省 · 黔东南苗族侗族自治州 · 古镇村落

## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

镇远古镇位于舞阳河畔，北岸为旧府城，南岸为旧卫城，两城池皆为明代所建，现尚存部分城墙和城门。城内外古建筑、传统民居、历史码头数量颇多。镇远历史悠久，自秦昭王三十年(公元前277年)设县开始至今已有近2300年的历史，其元代和清代为道、府所在地达700多年之久。

### English

Zhenyuan Ancient Town is located on the bank of Wuyang River, with the old Fucheng on the north bank and the old Acropolis on the south bank. Both cities were built in the Ming Dynasty, and some of the city walls and gates still exist. There are a lot of ancient buildings, traditional houses, and historical docks inside and outside the city. Zhenyuan has a long history. It has a history of nearly 2,300 years since the county was established in the 30th year of King Zhao of Qin (277 BC). It has been the seat of Taoism and government for more than 700 years in the Yuan and Qing dynasties.
