## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

科英布拉大学及其学院坐落在俯瞰城市的山上，在老城区内发展和发展了七个多世纪。著名的大学建筑包括 12 世纪的圣克鲁斯大教堂和一些 16 世纪的学院、自 1537 年以来一直是大学所在地的阿尔卡索瓦皇宫、具有丰富巴洛克风格装饰的乔安妮图书馆、18 世纪的植物园和大学出版社，以及 1940 年代创建的大型“大学城”。该大学的建筑成为葡语世界其他高等教育机构发展的参考，对学术和文学也产生了重大影响。科英布拉是综合性大学城的杰出典范，具有特定的城市类型以及多年来一直保持的仪式和文化传统。

### English

Situated on a hill overlooking the city, the University of Coimbra with its colleges grew and evolved over more than seven centuries within the old town. Notable university buildings include the 12 th century Cathedral of Santa Cruz and a number of 16 th century colleges, the Royal Palace of Alcáçova, which has housed the University since 1537, the Joanine Library with its rich baroque decor, the 18 th century Botanical Garden and University Press, as well as the large “University City” created during the 1940s. The University’s edifices became a reference in the development of other institutions of higher education in the Portuguese-speaking world where it also exerted a major influence on learning and literature. Coimbra offers an outstanding example of an integrated university city with a specific urban typology as well as its own ceremonial and cultural traditions that have been kept alive through the ages.
