## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

该剧院由两部分组成：贝伦的和平剧院和共和广场，以及马瑙斯的亚马逊剧院和圣塞巴斯蒂安广场。这些建筑建于亚马逊“黄金时代”，是 1879 年至 1912 年间橡胶周期经济高峰时期的巴西帕拉州和亚马孙州。这些建筑群受到法国和欧洲建筑的启发，装饰着令人想起亚马逊的图案，被设计成政治和城市野心的象征。和平剧院和共和广场分别于 1878 年和 1889 年落成，彰显了贝伦市作为港口和州首府的战略地位，并庆祝了巴西共和国的建立；而于 1896 年落成的亚马逊剧院是橡胶繁荣时期马瑙斯大规模城市发展的一部分，与圣塞巴斯蒂安广场一起体现了贝伦市的财富和繁荣。 prestige of the future capital of the Amazon.

### English

The property consists of two component parts: The Theatro da Paz and the Praça da República in Belém, and the Teatro Amazonas and the Largo de São Sebastião in Manaus. These were built in the States of Pará and Amazonas in Brazil during the Amazonian ‘Golden Age’ as a result of the economic peak of the Rubber Cycle between 1879 and 1912. Inspired by French and European architecture and decorated with motifs evoking the Amazon, these ensembles were designed as symbols of political and urban ambition. Inaugurated in 1878 and 1889, the Theatro da Paz and the Praça da República asserted the strategic role of the city of Belém as a port and State capital and celebrated the Brazilian Republic, while the Teatro Amazonas, inaugurated in 1896, was part of the large-scale urban development of Manaus during the rubber boom and, along with the Largo de São Sebastião, expressed the wealth and prestige of the future capital of the Amazon.
