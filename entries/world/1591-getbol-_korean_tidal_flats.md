## 简介 / Introduction

### 中文

该遗产位于黄海东部，由韩国西南部、南部、西部海岸的多种滩涂生态系统组成。此次扩展通过纳入新的组成部分，扩大原有系列遗产的范围，进一步代表河口型、开阔海岸型、群岛型、半封闭型滩涂的多样性。遗产地在地质、海洋、气候条件的复杂共同作用下形成，呈现多种地貌特征，包括沙嘴、贝壳堤、潮道、潮沟及众多岛屿。这些高生产力的生态系统维系着复杂的生态群落，滋养了极为丰富的潮滩无脊椎动物多样性，并为候鸟以及特有和受威胁物种提供了关键栖息地。

### English

The property comprises six component parts located along the southwestern, southern, and western coasts of the Republic of Korea on the eastern shore of the Yellow Sea. Originally inscribed in 2021 and extended in 2026, it encompasses a diverse system of tidal flat ecosystems, including estuarine, open-coast, archipelagic, and semi-enclosed tidal flats shaped by a complex combination of geological, oceanographic, and climatic conditions. These highly productive coastal wetlands support exceptional biodiversity, with 2,454 recorded plant and animal species, including a remarkable diversity of tidal-flat invertebrates and 53 species endemic to the Yellow Sea. Situated at the heart of the East Asian-Australasian Flyway, the property provides critical feeding, breeding, and stopover habitats for migratory waterbirds, supporting 216 bird species, including 24 globally threatened species and important populations of species such as the Spoon-billed Sandpiper, Far Eastern Curlew, and Black-faced Spoonbill. The property also supports 114 fish species, two species of marine mammals, and diverse communities of benthic diatoms, macroalgae, halophytes, and macrobenthos.
